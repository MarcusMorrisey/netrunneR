# .claude/plans/deck-compare/

Planner state for Deck Compare (the ice/breaker app's NetrunnerDB decklist
fetch and pairing feature). Written by the planner, moved here from a temp
directory; treat as a record of what was decided, not as a live document.

## Files

| File | What | When to read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `plan.json` | The authoritative plan: 5 milestones, decisions `DL-031`..`DL-040`, per-milestone acceptance criteria | Checking what a milestone requires, or what a decision id says |
| `context.json` | The context the plan was built from: task spec, constraints, entry points, rejected alternatives | Understanding why an approach was ruled out before proposing it again |
| `qr-plan-design.json` | The quality-review items the plan was checked against | Reviewing whether a plan change breaks a review criterion |
| `architect-findings.md` | Findings from the plan-design QR remediation pass -- what the first review round caught and how the plan was revised in response | Understanding why the plan looks the way it does, not just what it says |
